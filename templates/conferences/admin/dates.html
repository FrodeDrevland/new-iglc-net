For example "Abstracts due" or "Full papers due". An extended deadline: put the new date under Date and the old one under Before extension, and the website shows the old one struck through.

Tick Main event for the parts of the conference itself (the welcome reception, the industry day, the conference days, the dinner, the PhD summer school...): they get a description and an icon, and the Main events block shows them as cards. The programme page shows them until the detailed programme is published.

Belongs to lets a page show only its own dates: an Important dates block set to "Papers (submission, review)" on the call for papers page shows the abstract and paper deadlines and the review dates, and nothing else.
