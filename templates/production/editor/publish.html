Publish the papers

A chief editor asks for publication; a publisher approves it. Each paper goes on the site with its DOI, its page numbers and the IGLC headers and footers. When the last one is published, the page numbers are fixed, the conference appears in the proceedings list and a ZIP of all papers is made. The full proceedings PDF is a later step.

The authors’ check of the details

Each paper’s authors get an email with a link to check its title, their names, affiliations and ORCID iDs as published and registered with Crossref, and to confirm or correct them. One answer per paper is enough. Corrections come to the paper’s page (and by email to the chief editors), where they are applied to the published record.

    DOIs at Crossref

    The papers’ DOIs work once Crossref has registered them. A deposit sends the metadata of all published papers (authors with affiliations and ORCID iDs, titles, abstracts, pages) and of the proceedings; sending a new one updates the records, for example after a correction or when the full proceedings get their ISBN.
